Read published numbers together with their host, toolchain, profile, cache state, and trial count. Do not generalize a small synchronous build to HTTP or SQLite, or call a short Actor measurement long-running server performance.

[Canonical measurement records](https://github.com/disnana/Nagi/blob/main/PERFORMANCE.md) include raw evidence and limitations.
